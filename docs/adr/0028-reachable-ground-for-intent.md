# Reachable ground for intent

A swarm may paint an Intent Grid cell when it contains ground a nanobot can reach from that swarm’s home with normal body clearance. Permanent Rock Formations determine this area for the lifetime of the scenario. A blocked home rejects the scenario. The same map governs brush strokes, agent commands and strategic planning. Brushes skip unreachable cells, programmatic paint rejects them, and strategic reviews remove invalid paint.

Paint marks where work is wanted. A nanobot still needs a navigable place to do that work. A partially open tile can accept paint and support work on its accessible side even when its center is blocked. If rock divides the tile, an extraction or delivery on the isolated side still needs an accessible interaction point. Construction also requires space for its footprint. Structures, deposits and other temporary obstacles affect individual routes and jobs without changing which cells can be painted.

Defenders use the same ground for pursuit and the usual attack range when firing. Threats and the Pursuit Halo retain the territorial rules in [Defender engagement](0025-defender-engagement.md).
