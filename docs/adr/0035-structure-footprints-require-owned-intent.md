# Structure footprints require owned intent

The placement intent must cover the entire Building Footprint for as long as a swarm wants to build, operate, or maintain a structure. Source Stockpiles require Gather, Sink Stockpiles and Production Facilities require Build, and Chargers require Defend. This gives paint erasure the same meaning for players and the Strategic Controller.

Losing any required paint cancels a Planned Structure, including during clearing. A completed structure stays solid and follows normal damage and decay rules, but stops its work, Maintenance, and mineral transfers. Full repainting before collapse restores eligibility and retains stored minerals, funded production progress, and output waiting for an exit. A painted plan still survives a drop in demand, and a painted Sink that no longer pays for its Maintenance can still be drawn down.
