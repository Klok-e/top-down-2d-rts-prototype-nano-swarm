# Economy-driven gathering and throughput-ranked logistics

Workers gathered whenever Gather work existed, because Gather pressure counts unclaimed deposit minerals (tens of thousands) against single units of construction and maintenance. Swarms banked hundreds of minerals in stockpiles while facilities starved and structures decayed. Gathering is now sized by the economy instead of by deposit contents.

- **Gather Quota.** Each swarm assigns `ceil(needed income / gather rate)` Workers to gathering. Needed income is Spending Capacity plus the gap to the Reserve Target, closed over `reserve_seconds`. Spending Capacity counts facilities that are producing or have unmet Population Demand at their full cycle rate, plus Defender charge upkeep. It measures what the swarm could spend, not what supply allows, so a starved swarm still asks for gatherers. The Reserve Target is `reserve_seconds` (default 10) of Spending Capacity, held in Source and Sink Stockpiles; Charger, hopper and cargo minerals are already committed and do not count.
- **Gather rate.** The rate is minerals extracted per productive gatherer-second over a trailing 20 s window. `gather_rate_fallback` applies until a full window exists, and again whenever a window measures no extraction or no productive gatherer-time. Gatherer-time, rather than the current count, keeps the rate steady while gatherers join or leave. A gatherer waiting at its deposit for Source room is excluded: counting it would read a full Source as slow Workers and raise the quota when more gatherers cannot help.
- **Bounds.** The quota keeps one gatherer per Gather-painted deposit, so income and rate measurement never stall. It stays within each deposit's work-ring body count, and within one gatherer per full load of Source Stockpile room plus that floor. Without the room cap, gatherers split scarce room into one-mineral loads, the measured rate collapsed, and the quota drove more Workers into the jam.
- **Enforcement.** Population Demand uses the quota for Gather Workers, replacing per-deposit ring slots. Allocation stops offering Gather once a swarm's gatherers reach the quota, and a deposit stops accepting gatherers once its ring is full. Gatherers leave only by attrition, re-entering allocation after each delivery, so a falling quota never strands cargo.
- **Haul ranking** (amends [ADR-0005](0005-tiered-logistics-flow.md)). Hauler terminal work is ranked by route cost per mineral carried plus `haul_urgency_cost` per urgency level, so a short full refill beats a long trip carrying a few minerals. Charger emergencies and fully aged terminals still form a strict tier ahead of that cost. A terminal draws only from Sink Stockpiles within two cells of its nearest stocked Sink. Otherwise a far pickup holds the terminal's claim for the whole trip while nearer stock sits unclaimed.
- **Waiting Haulers.** A Production Facility that is producing, or idle with unmet demand, offers input space beyond its hopper: `waiting_hauler_loads` (default 2) full Hauler loads, and at least one production cycle. Haulers may claim that space and wait for the hopper to drain, so the next cycle starts on arrival instead of after a fresh trip. They unload only into real free space and re-route if the facility goes idle. With a 5-mineral hopper and 10-mineral loads, a single queued cycle let only one Hauler serve a facility per round trip; two waiting loads cut starved facility-seconds from 530 to 384 per swarm over seeds 11-14. A larger hopper does about as well (383), but slow Hauler transfer and the small hopper are intended balance, so the queue grows instead.

  A throughput-sized Haul Quota was tried and rejected: Hauler demand ran away to hundreds, Defenders fell below ten, and delivery to terminals did not rise, because the hopper, not the Hauler count, was the limit.
- **Maintenance and Build handoff** (amends [ADR-0009](0009-regional-intent-allocation.md)). Each such job holds one Worker, and the first idle Worker anywhere claimed it while Workers beside the structure kept gathering. An idle Worker at least twice as close takes over the job, saving at least four cells of travel, while the holder has not started working.

## Considered Options

- **Measured consumption instead of Spending Capacity.** Rejected: a starved swarm spends little, so its quota would stay low and lock in the starvation.
- **On/off gathering around a stock threshold.** Rejected: it overshoots both ways. A proportional quota with attrition responds smoothly.
- **Releasing gatherers immediately when the quota drops.** Rejected: it needs cargo handling, and the gain is small, since trips are short.
