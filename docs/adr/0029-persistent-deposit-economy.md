# Maintain a persistent economy at owned Resource Deposits

[Mine Saturation](0038-gate-expansion-on-mine-saturation.md) revises the admission rules for new mines. The [objective eligibility decision](0036-check-strategic-objective-eligibility.md) defines when expansion and military objectives can proceed together.

Keep the Strategic Controller's mining commitments across military reviews and expansion. Adding a neutral Resource Deposit increases the number of operating mines. Exhaustion, loss of access, or loss of the Resource Deposit removes that site's support while other mines stay active. A surviving mine keeps Gather paint, an adjacent Build Zone and compact Defend coverage, and a Logistics Corridor along the navigable connection home.

When no owned mine faces a Threat, add at most one free neutral deposit per review. Give a cold first mine up to thirty simulated seconds to begin producing before opening another site, so a limited crew can establish supply. A stocked local Source Stockpile establishes that the first mine has supply. A crew of five reachable living Workers can staff extraction and Source Stockpile construction at both sites and build a shared Sink Stockpile. Four suffice once the first Source Stockpile is built, allowing expansion before extraction begins. Once extraction starts or the grace period expires, expansion can proceed while work continues at established sites. Estimate its value from the extractable reserve, distance from home, hauling route, and exposure. Reserve paint for the entire mine before committing to it. The military front can contest a different neutral deposit during expansion. Free deposits take priority over raids and home pushes. The admission limit leaves paint room for existing commitments. Longer routes for already owned mines may use the larger limit set by the edit reserve. If a complete revised plan exceeds that limit, retry while retaining the last complete plan.

After the first expansion, reserve Worker capacity for extraction and unfinished Source Stockpiles at existing mines and shared upkeep. A new mine needs capacity for extraction and construction unless a usable Source Stockpile is already nearby. Existing haul routes increase the cost of committing another mine according to the living Hauler fleet. A deposit that exceeds the available workforce remains free, so the swarm keeps investing in its economy until it can support expansion or needs to respond to a Threat.

An owned Resource Deposit is an economic commitment expressed through the swarm's Gather intent. Neutral deposits still share one finite resource pool under [independent swarm intent](0018-independent-swarm-intent.md). Scenario-assigned exclusive deposits retain their original extraction eligibility. Enemy Gather paint makes a neutral deposit ineligible for peaceful expansion. Overlap at an established mine leaves both swarms' orders intact.

A separate economy plan avoids the repeated disruption of picking a single preferred Resource Deposit whenever the controller changes its military objective. The controller still only paints and erases its own orders. Extraction, construction, and transport follow the same rules for both swarms.
