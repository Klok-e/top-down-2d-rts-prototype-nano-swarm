# Construction preserves friendly access

Automatic Construction preserves every previously connected pair of friendly structure and eligible Resource Deposit interaction areas, with clearance for their users, and keeps the new site reachable by its builder. Placement and pre-completion checks consider all Planned Structures as completed together, preventing individually safe commitments from jointly sealing access without requiring disconnected networks to join or protecting enemy routes. A plan failing its final check is cancelled rather than left pending, extending ADR-0011's stable commitment lifecycle.

## Consequences

- Ordinary plans remain traversable. After construction work and a successful access check, Structure Clearing bars entrants while occupants leave; activation waits until clear. While access remains valid, crowd congestion may delay clearing indefinitely without timeout cancellation: temporary occupancy delays construction rather than invalidating its site.
- During clearing, repeat the combined-plan friendly access check after relevant blocker or plan layout changes and immediately before activation. A failed check cancels the plan through the same cancellation lifecycle, so delayed activation cannot rely on stale access validation.
- Finishing construction work immediately releases the assigned Worker for other work. Structure Clearing requires no continuing Worker assignment, so congestion cannot indefinitely occupy construction labor.
- Cancellation immediately releases the worker, capacity commitment, and reserved footprint. A brief red pulse and collapsing, fading outline communicate the loss without text; the effect reserves no space. Construction costs Worker time, so cancellation requires no mineral refund.
- The cancelled site remains excluded until blocker or plan layout changes; cancellation's own removal cannot immediately clear that exclusion. Continued demand can choose another valid site under existing demand rules, and Production Facility cancellation starts a fresh pressure window.
- Structure placement protects useful friendly connections rather than all empty space or enemy access. Nanobot crowds are handled through local avoidance and clearing rather than static placement rejection.
