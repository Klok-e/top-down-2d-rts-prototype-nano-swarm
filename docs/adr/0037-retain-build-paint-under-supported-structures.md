# Retain Build paint under supported structures

Across reviews, the Strategic Controller retains each Build cell required by a Planned Structure or a completed structure it intends to keep. It decides where to offer space for future construction independently of those commitments, so an idle Production Facility or an advancing Staging Point cannot accidentally withdraw support. Retiring a structure or losing access to its footprint can end that support under the [owned footprint rules](0035-structure-footprints-require-owned-intent.md).

A completed Production Facility retains its Build support for as long as its footprint remains reachable. This keeps installed capacity available when mineral delivery recovers.

The controller offers a small, stable allowance of unused Build room at home and at Owned Resource Deposits. Production Pressure determines when that room is needed for construction.
