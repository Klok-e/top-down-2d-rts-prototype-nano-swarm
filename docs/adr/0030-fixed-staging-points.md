# Advance military fronts through fixed staging points

The [Staging Point arrival decision](0039-count-assigned-defenders-at-staging-points.md) specifies which Defenders count toward arrival and when an active raid holds its point.

Following the median Defender position made paint creep and shift sideways as the force moved. The Strategic Controller chooses one military objective and records a short sequence of staging points along its reachable approach. The active Defend block moves to the next point when the configured fraction of living Defenders has arrived, so the front can lead the force and stay fixed between arrivals.

Permanent terrain clips a Defend block to ground connected to its anchor through neighboring cells in the block. Defenders can assemble at the staging point or at home without having to go around terrain outside the block.

A hunt reuses its destination while the target stays in the destination area. Once the target leaves that area, the controller plans the remaining approach from the active staging point. Movement within the painted block preserves paint, and a covered attack gets one stalled review to resume progress before a stronger objective can replace it.

An unexhausted enemy mine with reachable Gather paint can draw a raid even when its Nanobots and structures are absent. The raid stages at the Gather area until the Defenders arrive. An undefended mine that has been reached yields priority to other objectives until hostile activity returns or the enemy removes its Gather claim.
