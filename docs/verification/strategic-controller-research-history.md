### First reserved numerical comparison

Use Timed as incumbent, the frozen Timed/v10 opponent pool, Narrows/Crossroads layouts, both physical starts, and eight reserved seeds: 107, 139, 211, 251, 307, 331, 401, and 443. Inspection of retained experiment manifests found no prior use of the two additional seeds, 401 and 443. The fixed endpoint is 128 valid game trials: two subjects for each of eight opponent/layout/side pairs in each seed block. Preserve technical failures and investigate before completing the same frozen matrix; do not drop inconvenient valid games or add optional observations to alter a decision. Each seed is one statistical block, not eight independent samples. For seed `i`, `C_i` and `I_i` are equal means of candidate and incumbent scored HP over the same eight strata, and `delta_i = C_i - I_i`.

Both numerical branches require a complete, attributable matrix and these observed joint-stratum guards, aggregated across seeds: candidate wins must be at least incumbent wins and candidate losses at most incumbent losses in every opponent/layout/side cell; candidate mean scored HP must be at least incumbent mean minus `max(200 HP, 10% of incumbent mean)`. This prevents equal win counts from concealing additional losses in place of unresolved games. Equal coverage makes the joint-cell win/loss guards imply the corresponding marginals and overall guards. These are conservative practical vetoes, not separate claims of stratum-level statistical significance.

- **Damage superiority:** Require grand raw mean damage gain of at least `max(200 HP, 10% of incumbent grand mean)` and all eight seed-block damage differences strictly positive. The exact one-sided sign probability is `1/256`, below the branch allocation of 0.025. The inference concerns consistent block direction/location under the sign-test assumptions; it is not a confidence interval for the raw population mean. The grand mean threshold is an additional observed practical requirement.
- **Close damage with faster wins:** If the superiority branch does not pass, require every `z_i = delta_i / max(I_i, 2000 HP)` strictly inside `(-0.05, +0.05)`, and absolute grand raw mean damage difference strictly below `max(100 HP, 5% of incumbent grand mean)`. The standardized block-location comparison uses two one-sided sign tests as an intersection-union test, each with allocation 0.025; it does not establish a mean-confidence interval. Additionally require all eight seed-block means of incumbent minus candidate cutoff-capped completion cost to be strictly positive; grand all-attempt cost improvement must be at least `max(30 seconds, 10% of incumbent mean cost)`, with no candidate cost regression in any joint stratum. Require at least eight jointly won matched pairs spanning at least six seeds, and a mean actual win-time improvement on those pairs of at least `max(30 seconds, 10% of incumbent joint-win mean)`. Every non-win costs the full 600 seconds in the all-attempt statistic; it never receives an invented win time. Do not perform the inferential sign test on an outcome-selected subset of seeds.

Allocate 0.025 to each alternative branch; the close-damage branch's equivalence sides and speed requirements are conjunctive. A zero block difference fails a strict-positive condition, and a comparability boundary fails the strict margin. Practical minimum-gain thresholds and nonregression guards are inclusive as stated. Report block observations, exact descriptive sign probabilities, raw HP means, all outcomes, joint-win counts, and every failed guard. Failure to satisfy either branch is not evidence of equivalence and cannot authorize promotion.
